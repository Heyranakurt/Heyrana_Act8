TRIP STORIES

MY NORTH CEBU TRIP


April 19, 2025

Maravilla Beach Resort, Tabuelan

I went to Maravilla Beach Resort with my cousins, and it was a trip full of good moments. We swam, took photos and spent the whole day together by the water. We also ate a lot of seafood, and every meal turned into more laughing and storytelling. It's a trip I'll remember for a long time.


April 21, 2025

Camping at Montealegre Falls, Tuburan

I went back to Montealegre Falls with my cousins, and this time we camped there. We brought so many snacks that we were never hungry, and we spent the trip together by the falls. It was a different kind of adventure from the beach, and one more trip I'll remember.


April 21, 2025

Hinagdanan Beach Resort, Catmon

My classmates and I won an event on campus, and we knew right away that we had to celebrate. We chose Hinagdanan Beach Resort in Catmon for the day, and everyone was excited to go. All the hard work we put into the event felt worth it once we arrived.

We spent time together by the water, talked about how the event went and laughed about the best parts. It felt good to celebrate as a team, because we had all worked for that win together. It's a celebration I'll remember for a long time.


May 19 - 21, 2026

Durhan White Beach, Tabuelan

I spent 2 nights at Durhan White Beach in Tabuelan with my BNS friends, and it was one of the most fun trips I've had. From the moment we arrived, we were already planning what to do first. We played a lot of games, and everyone kept the energy up from morning until night.

In between, we did some grinding together, which somehow made the trip even more memorable. Staying overnight meant we had extra time to talk, laugh and enjoy each other's company. By the end of the second day, none of us wanted to leave.


MY SOUTH CEBU TRIP


August 21, 2023

Whale Shark Watching, Tan-awan, Oslob

Our South Cebu trip started early in Oslob, because the whale sharks are best seen in the morning. The sea was calm and the sky was just starting to brighten, and we were all excited to see them. The moment we arrived in Tan-awan, the nervous feeling turned into pure excitement.

Seeing the gentle giants glide through the water so close to us was unforgettable. They were huge, but so calm and graceful that all our worries disappeared. After the experience, we had a hearty breakfast and talked about the whale sharks the whole time.


January 25, 2024

Kawasan Falls, Matutinao, Badian

Next, we went to Kawasan Falls in Badian to cool off after a long ride. The turquoise water looked even more beautiful in person, and the fresh air and lush greenery made the whole place feel peaceful. It felt like we had stepped into a postcard.

We swam, took lots of photos and enjoyed the cool water together. We ate a satisfying meal afterwards, which tasted even better after all that swimming. It was one of my favorite stops of the whole trip.


September 5, 2025

Osmeña Peak, Mantalongon, Dalaguete

At Osmeña Peak in Dalaguete, we hiked up to see the rolling green hills with the sea far below. The fresh, cool air was a nice change from the beaches, and the climb was more fun because we laughed along the way. Every step felt like it was leading to something special.

When we reached the top, the view took our breath away. We stayed for a while, took photos with the clouds above us and simply enjoyed the quiet. The hike was tiring, but the view made it all worth it.


July 7, 2026

Panagsama Beach, Moalboal

We ended the trip in Moalboal, a place that has been on my bucket list for a long time. The clear water and the relaxed beach vibe were the perfect way to finish a busy few days. After so much adventure, it felt good to slow down.

We swam, relaxed and took in the view, and it was one of those moments I didn't want to end. This trip showed me how different the south side of Cebu is from the north. It's a trip I'll remember for a long time.

Made with love for Cebu.
